Base64 Encoding and Decoding

Encode text as Base64 and decode Base64 strings.

Purpose

Encode text as Base64 and decode Base64 strings.

Exports registered by the module initializer

ExportRole
encodeencode helper
decodedecode helper

Usage

import gs_base64;
define encoded = gs_base64.encode("greenServeFE");
print(encoded);
print(gs_base64.decode(encoded));
Base64 is an encoding, not encryption; it provides no confidentiality or integrity.
