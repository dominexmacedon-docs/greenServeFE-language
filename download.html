ENGLISH · FOR SCHOOLS AND EDUCATORS

Schools are welcome to adopt these materials

Schools, teachers, educators and programming clubs are welcome to use the greenServeFE Grade 6–12 textbooks for educational learning, classroom teaching, student practice and school programming activities. You may share the official download page with students and families so they can access the matching grade-level PDF.

For a consistent learning experience, schools can select the appropriate grade level, use the PDF as a classroom reference, and combine the lessons with supervised hands-on programming exercises. Please retain the author and project attribution when redistributing the materials.
